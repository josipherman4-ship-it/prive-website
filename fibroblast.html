How Fibroblast Works

Precise plasma dots stimulate the skin to produce new collagen and elastin, so the treated area visibly tightens over time. No laser, no injectables.

  1. Stage One

    Controlled Plasma Dot

    A precise plasma dot on the upper skin layer sends heat deeper, triggering natural repair.

  2. Stage Two

    New Collagen & Elastin

    The skin responds by building new collagen and elastin, the proteins that decline with age.

  3. Stage Three

    Visible Tightening

    Over roughly 6 to 12 weeks, skin looks tighter and smoother, with no surgical recovery. Results fade and need maintenance.

What Fibroblast Can Treat

The treatment can be performed on any loose or saggy area of the body. From the delicate eye area to the jawline, neck and body.

Questions Worth Asking Before You Enroll

If your question isn't here, ask us directly. We'd rather answer it now than have you guess.

Do I need to already be a licensed beauty professional to enroll?

No. The course is built for working beauty professionals, practitioners who trained before, and serious newcomers willing to put in the work. Your certificate depends on your trainer reviewing your practical results, not on the experience you arrive with.

Who may perform Fibroblast is decided where you work, not by us or any certificate. Confirm your local requirements and your insurer before you treat clients.

Isn't Fibroblast just another trend that's going to become oversaturated?

It's the right question. A treatment gets popular, short courses flood the market, results disappoint, and everyone competes on price.

Proper training is the answer to that: being the practitioner in your area who picks the right candidates, sets honest expectations about downtime, and gets results that bring clients back. What we can't promise is demand in your specific area. Nobody honestly can.

Is online training enough, or do I need in-person practice?

Not if nobody checks your work, and that's the gap this course closes. You see every technique in 6+ hours of training and 4+ hours of filmed treatment demonstrations. You practise on an orange first, then move on to a real model once your technique is sound, with a Master Trainer reviewing your results until they're consistent.

This is an online course. To train in a room with a trainer, see Live Trainings.

Will my professional insurance cover this treatment?

That depends on your insurer, your policy and where you work, so we can't answer it for you. Standard beauty policies often don't include Plasma Fibroblast. Ask your insurer in writing whether your policy covers Plasma Fibroblast by name. Being insured and being permitted to perform the treatment are separate questions. Confirm both before your first client.

Our Story

From Microblading To Plasma-Fibroblast

Privé Academy began operations in December 2010. In its early years, the academy built its reputation teaching and practicing Microblading, becoming a recognized leader in the European beauty field.

Everything changed when founder Samo Sambol spent years developing a new method: Plasma-Fibroblast. After successfully introducing it to the world of beauty as a non-surgical alternative for skin tightening and rejuvenation, Privé shifted its focus — training hundreds of new Fibroblast specialists and developing a proprietary product line to support them.

The world of beauty moves quickly. Fifteen years in, we're still teaching the same way we started: real technique, real science, and the judgment to know when to use it.

Founder & CEO

Samo Sambol

Samo founded Privé Academy in December 2010, building its early reputation on Microblading before becoming the Master Trainer who successfully introduced and popularized the Plasma-Fibroblast treatment across the European beauty market.

Since then, he's trained over 1,000 specialists worldwide and built a proprietary, CE-marked product line.

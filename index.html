02 — The Science

What Is Fibroblast?

A 100% natural, non-surgical technique for skin tightening and rejuvenation.

Plasma-Fibroblast uses ionized atmospheric air to create a controlled electric arc just above the skin's surface. The arc never touches the skin directly — instead it transmits heat into the upper layers, triggering the body's own healing response. Over the following 6–8 weeks, that response stimulates new collagen and elastin production, gradually tightening and resurfacing the treated area. Sessions run 60–90 minutes, most clients need around two treatments, and results typically last 2–4 years.

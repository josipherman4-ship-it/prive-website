Brand Guidelines · Colors, Type & Logo Usage
01 — Palette

Brand Colors

Navy carries authority and structure. Alice Blue and white keep the page light and editorial. Night Rider is the working text color. Mustard is used sparingly — a single accent, never a background.

Navy
#013559
Use for — headlines, primary buttons, dark section backgrounds, logo on light.
Alice Blue
#EFF8FF
Use for — alternating section backgrounds, soft panels behind navy content.
Night Rider
#2B2B2B
Use for — body copy, footer background, logo on light when navy is already in use.
Mustard
#C0B905
Accent only — underlines, eyebrows, dividers, button outlines. Never a large fill.

03 — Components

Buttons

Two shapes carry two different weights of action — a filled pill for the primary path, and a squared, mustard-edged outline for a secondary or quieter action.

Primary
Navy fill · white text · full pill radius (50px) · no shadow. For the one action per screen that matters most.
Secondary
Transparent fill · navy text · mustard outline · square corners. For a lighter-weight or alternate action.
04 — Logo

Logo Usage

The sunburst mark is white by default — built for dark backgrounds. On light backgrounds, recolor to Navy or Night Rider. Never place the white mark directly on white.

White mark — on Navy
Night Rider mark — on White / Alice Blue

Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least the height of the sunburst rays. Do not stretch, rotate, or recolor the mark to any color outside Navy, Night Rider, or White.

The Technique

Fibroblast Skin-Tightening Is One Of The Leading Natural Anti-Aging Treatments In The World.

The technique works by creating a small, controlled electric arc that stimulates the skin to produce new collagen and elastin fibres — causing the treated area to visibly contract and tighten over time. It's not a laser, and it's not an injectable. It's a targeted, non-invasive stimulation of your client's own skin.

Stage One

Controlled Micro-Arc

A precise, superficial point of contact with the upper dermal layer transmits heat to the lower levels of the skin, triggering the body's natural repair response.

Stage Two

New Collagen & Elastin

This response causes the skin to generate new collagen and elastin fibres — the same structural proteins that naturally decline as we age.

Stage Three

Visible Tightening

Collagen continues to build and remodel over roughly 6 to 12 weeks. The result: improved texture, reduced wrinkles, visibly tighter skin — no incisions, no anesthesia, no surgical recovery. Results are not permanent. They fade and need maintenance, and any training that tells you otherwise is selling you something.

A treatment is only as good as the precision behind it. That's exactly why the equipment and the training you build it on matter as much as the science itself — which is where Privé comes in.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Some are needed for the site to work. Others help us understand how the site is used or measure our advertising, and we only set those if you say yes.

Your choice

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics and marketing cookies or only the necessary ones. Nothing optional is set until you choose. You can change your mind at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.

Necessary

These keep the site working and remember your cookie choice. They don't need consent.

Controlling cookies in your browser

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block necessary cookies, forms and checkout may not work.
